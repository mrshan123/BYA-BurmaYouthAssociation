Governance

BYA will progressively publish its organizational structure, leadership responsibilities, policies and program ownership as the organization expands.

Financial Responsibility

Public financial reporting should distinguish income, restricted funds, program spending, administration and material commitments where applicable.

Data & Privacy

Volunteer and contact information should be collected only for defined purposes, protected appropriately and never exposed publicly without consent.

Safeguarding

As BYA works with young people, safeguarding, respectful conduct and responsible communication should be treated as core organizational standards.

BUILDING IN PUBLIC

Policies will be published as they become formally adopted.

This page intentionally separates current commitments from future formal policies. BYA should not claim to have a policy, certification or governance mechanism until it has actually been adopted and can be documented.
