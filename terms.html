Terms of Use

These terms describe the basic conditions for using BYA's public website and resources.

Public information

BYA aims to publish useful and accurate information, but external resources, opportunities and links may change. Verify important details with the original provider before applying or paying.

External services

Learning platforms, job sites and other third-party websites are independent services. A BYA link does not by itself mean that BYA endorses every service, employer or outcome.

Responsible use

Do not misuse BYA forms, impersonate BYA, submit malicious content, request money from other participants without authorization, or use public resources to harm others.

Youth safety

Participation must follow BYA's safeguarding and respectful-conduct expectations. Safety concerns should be reported through the available official contact route.

Changes

BYA may update these terms as its operations develop. The website should display the current version and review date when the organization establishes a formal policy-control process.
