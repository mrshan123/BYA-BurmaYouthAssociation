BYA • PROGRAMS & AREAS OF WORK

Turning youth potential into public value.

မြန်မာလူငယ်များအတွက် ပညာရေး၊ အလုပ်အကိုင်၊ Digital Skills၊ Community နှင့် လူငယ်ဖွံ့ဖြိုးရေးကို ချိတ်ဆက်ပေးမည့် BYA ၏ public-benefit platform ဖြစ်သည်။

OUR FRAMEWORK

Six connected areas of work

BYA သည် project တစ်ခုတည်းကိုသာ မဟုတ်ဘဲ လူငယ်တစ်ဦး၏ learning → skills → opportunity → contribution လမ်းကြောင်းတစ်ခုလုံးကို support လုပ်ရန် ရည်ရွယ်ထားသည်။

01 • LEARNING

Learning & Education

Practical learning resources, study guidance, digital literacy and self-development pathways.

02 • CAREER

Career Development

Career readiness, workplace skills, CV guidance, interview preparation and professional growth.

03 • DIGITAL

Digital & IT Skills

Technology awareness and practical digital capability for education, work and community participation.

04 • COMMUNITY

Community & Volunteerism

A pathway for young people to contribute skills, ideas and time to meaningful community initiatives.

05 • RESEARCH

Research & Education Development

Youth-focused research, knowledge sharing and evidence-informed educational initiatives as BYA grows.

06 • OPPORTUNITY

Opportunity Access

A structured gateway for scholarships, internships, volunteering, jobs and other verified opportunities.

BUILDING WITH INTEGRITY

We will publish impact only when it can be verified.

BYA is a growing youth organization. We will not manufacture member counts, beneficiary numbers or success statistics. As programs become active, verified outcomes, methodology and reporting will be published transparently.
