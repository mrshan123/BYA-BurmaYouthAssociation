What we may collect

  • Name and contact details when voluntarily submitted.
  • Volunteer skills and areas of interest.
  • Messages or collaboration enquiries.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate the website.

How information should be used

  • Responding to enquiries and volunteer applications.
  • Operating and improving BYA programs.
  • Maintaining safe and accountable administration.
  • Providing information the user has requested.

Data principles

BYA should minimize collection, restrict access, protect confidential information, avoid unnecessary sharing and provide a clear way to ask about personal information. Formal retention periods and legal notices should be published when BYA establishes the relevant operating processes.

Important: Never submit passwords, banking credentials, one-time codes or highly sensitive personal information through a public BYA form.
