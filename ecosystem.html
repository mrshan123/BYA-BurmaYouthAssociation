OPERATING MODEL

From website to organization.

BYA's long-term architecture should connect public content with real operating processes.

People

Member, volunteer, mentor, youth leader and partner profiles.

Programs

Programs with objectives, eligibility, delivery model and outcomes.

Data

Structured records, consent, reporting periods and evidence trails.

Trust

Safeguarding, privacy, accessibility, complaints and governance.

Partners

Partnership pipeline, agreements, deliverables and disclosure.

Impact

Outputs → outcomes → stories → evidence → annual reporting.

IMPORTANT

International standard does not mean invented scale.

BYA will grow by publishing verified facts: real programs, real opportunities, real partnerships, real impact and real governance. This is the foundation for credibility with youth, institutions and future international partners.
