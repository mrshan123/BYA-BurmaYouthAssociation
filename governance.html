GOVERNANCE & LEADERSHIP

A governance model designed to grow with BYA.

BYA is developing a clear separation between strategic oversight, executive operations, independent advice and youth leadership.

ORGANIZATIONAL ARCHITECTURE

Four governance layers

01 • BOARD

Board of Directors

Strategic oversight, fiduciary responsibility, risk management and accountability.

Structure: Developing
02 • EXECUTIVE

Executive Team

Program delivery, operations, finance, safeguarding, partnerships and organizational management.

Structure: Developing
03 • ADVISORY

Advisory Board

Independent expertise in education, youth development, technology, careers and research.

Structure: Developing
04 • YOUTH

Youth Leadership

Youth representatives who inform programs, community priorities and lived-experience feedback.

Structure: Developing
CURRENT LEADERSHIP

Founder / Architect of Youth Development

BYA was founded in 2026. The governance layers above are an organizational framework for future appointments; no board, adviser or partner is represented as appointed until the appointment is formally confirmed.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Decision and oversight principles

  • Document major organizational decisions and delegated responsibilities.
  • Separate program delivery from oversight as the organization grows.
  • Declare and manage conflicts of interest.
  • Protect youth participants and confidential information.
  • Publish verified governance and impact information rather than estimates.
