Respect

No harassment, humiliation, discrimination or exploitation.

Boundaries

Volunteers and leaders should maintain appropriate professional boundaries.

Privacy

Personal information and private conversations should be handled responsibly.

Reporting & escalation

As BYA formalizes its safeguarding structure, it should publish named reporting channels, response responsibilities, confidentiality boundaries and escalation procedures. Until then, concerns should be raised through the official BYA contact channel.
