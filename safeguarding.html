SAFEGUARDING & PROTECTION

Safety is part of the system.

BYA's safeguarding architecture is designed for youth-facing activities. It must be operationalized before programs involving direct youth interaction are activated.

Policy & Code

Clear safeguarding policy, code of conduct, prohibited conduct, responsibilities and escalation rules.

Report a Concern

Future secure reporting route with confidential handling, defined response times and restricted access.

Confidentiality

Safeguarding records must be separated from ordinary member records and accessible only to authorized roles.

Anonymous Reporting

Where technically and legally appropriate, provide a reporting route that does not require identity disclosure.

Response & Escalation

Document triage, risk assessment, referral, escalation, decision logging and case closure.

Anti-Retaliation

Protect people who raise concerns in good faith and clearly explain how retaliation is handled.

Production gate: Before collecting safeguarding reports, BYA should designate an authorized safeguarding officer, define response procedures, secure the reporting channel, establish retention rules and publish appropriate referral/emergency information.
