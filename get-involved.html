VOLUNTEER PRINCIPLES

Professional, inclusive and responsible.

  • Respect young people's dignity, privacy and diverse backgrounds.
  • Use truthful information and cite sources where appropriate.
  • Do not request money from applicants on behalf of BYA without formal authorization.
  • Protect confidential information shared during volunteer work.
  • Build useful public resources, not personal promotion alone.
